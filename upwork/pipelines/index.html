One face,every angle.

Client Andrey DraganTest $300 · Stage 1 $500Run on Comfy Cloud

The rule that made it passidentity

Identity comes only from the LoRA. Reference images were used to train it and never at render time, so anyone with the JSON, the models and the seed gets the same woman back on their own machine.

The face is synthetic: the base prompt describes a generic young woman, no real person behind it.

#02Face LoRAR&D · Apr – May 2025

My face,as a model.

Dataset 23 selfiesTrained on ShakkerRendered on my Mac

To learn what a LoRA can hold, I trained one on myself: 23 cropped phone selfies, a FLUX LoRA trained on Shakker, saved at epoch six.

Then I ran it in ComfyUI on my own laptop, a quantised FLUX (GGUF Q4) with an 8-step Hyper-FLUX LoRA so a render fits in 18 GB. The picks went to a cloud GPU for the 4K and 8K finish.

#03Region editsR&D · Apr – May 2025

Find it, mask it,repaint it.

Florence-2 findsSAM2 masksFLUX Fill repaints

Hand-masking doesn’t scale. In this graph Florence-2 finds the garment from one word, SAM2 cuts the mask, and FLUX.1 Fill repaints only that region. The face, the hands and the light stay the original pixels.

  1. Load imageAny photo, no manual maskimage →
  2. Florence-2Grounded detection from the word “clothes”bbox →
  3. SAM2Box to a pixel mask, grown and featheredmask →
  4. FLUX.1 FillInpaints only the mask from a short promptimage →
  5. CompositePasted back over the untouched originaloutput

#05UGC ad graphR&D · Comfy Cloud · Jan 2026

One product photo,one creator ad.

36 nodes5 frames · 5 clips40 s out, with sound

Creator-style videos sell, but every one costs a creator, a day and a product in the post. This graph needs only the product photo.

Gemini 3 Pro Image writes five 9:16 selfie frames of one person holding it, each frame locked to the one before (same face, same room, same product). Veo 3.1 turns each into eight seconds with speech and sound, and the graph joins the five clips into one 40-second ad. The final cut is mine, in Premiere.

  1. Load imageThe product photo, nothing elseimage →
  2. Gemini 3 Pro Image ×5Chained 9:16 selfie frames, identity locked to frame one5 frames →
  3. Veo 3.1 Fast ×58 s each, generated speech and sound5 clips →
  4. Combine videoFrames, audio and fps joined in one subgraphvideo →
  5. 40-second adThen a tight cut in Premiereoutput

#06Product filmR&D · Comfy Cloud · Jan 2026

A sneaker shoot,no studio.

Seedream 4.5 ×9Hailuo ×953 s film

One phone photo of a sneaker, held over a rug. Nine Seedream 4.5 prompts turn it into nine studio key frames: rim light, a macro on the gold foil, a black colourway, each 2048 px tall.

Nine MiniMax Hailuo nodes then move the camera through each frame for six seconds at 1080p, and the graph strings them into one product film.

#07Photo booth workflowPaid test · Faruk Hossain · Sep 2025

A fan, a player,one locker room.

Segmind · Seedream 4.0$0.035 per image3 workflows

The brief, for a football photo booth: a fan stands in front of a camera, and the workflow keeps their real body (height, build, posture) and places them beside a fixed player photo in the club’s locker room, real enough to print. The tools were set: Nano Banana or Seedream, built on Segmind.

I built three workflows (women, kids, men). The fan’s photo and the player template go into Seedream 4.0 at 4K with one instruction; nothing is cut out by hand. Each image costs about three and a half cents.

  1. Fan photoCamera or upload, full bodyimage →
  2. Player templateThe club’s locker room and playerimage →
  3. Instruction“Put them together in locker room, side-by-side pose”text →
  4. Seedream 4.0 · 4KOne pass, the fan’s real proportions keptoutput

Once the eight-angle graph worked, I moved the idea out of ComfyUI into small browser apps built in Google AI Studio on Gemini’s image models. OmniView keeps the same eight camera presets as the graph; NanoCanvas does the 4K finish; GolfLens turns one product shot into a set of campaign angles.

#09Local modelsR&D · Sep 2026

Local,on a laptop.

MacBook Pro 18 GBQwen Image 2.1Q4 GGUF · 4-bit encoder

Some work should never leave the machine. I set up Qwen Image 2.1 on my MacBook Pro (Apple silicon, 18 GB shared memory): a Q4 GGUF model, a 4-bit text encoder, and notes in the graph explaining every choice.

Two things stood in the way, and both are fixed.

comfyui.log · 25 Sep 2026
[WARNING] [MPS-Conv3d-Fix] native MPS conv3d is broken on this system; using exact conv2d decomposition.
…
[INFO] Prompt executed in 00:34:02

1 · macOS 26 broke it. The Mac’s 3D convolution returned wrong numbers, so every image decoded as flat grey. A small startup patch now tests it and, only if it’s broken, routes it through an exact 2D path. Written in an AI-assisted session I directed.

2 · It was slow. A standard run took 10 to 34 minutes. With an 8-step speed LoRA at strength 2.0 the same graph runs in about four, which makes local work usable.

Knowing the ceiling

Not everything belongs on a laptop. In April 2025 Wan 2.1 at 14B ran out of memory here, so video and 8K finishing stay on cloud GPUs (Nordy, Comfy Cloud), and the laptop does private work and R&D.
